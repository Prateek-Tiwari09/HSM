Engineering Excellence in Fluid Power Since 2015

Incorporated in 2015 in Peenya, Bangalore, HSM Hydro Control Pvt. Ltd. has grown into a trusted single-window hydraulic partner for industrial manufacturing across India.

OUR STORY

A Single-Window Hydraulic Solution Provider

Founded in 2015, HSM Hydro Control Pvt. Ltd. was established to solve a pressing challenge in the manufacturing industry: the lack of timely, competent hydraulic engineering and repair support. Operating from our manufacturing setup in Peenya 2nd Stage, Bangalore, with branch support in Pune, we serve a wide range of industrial customers across India.

We specialize in custom hydraulic power pack fabrication, high-pressure test rig manufacturing, ISO-standard hydraulic cylinder builds, manifold block machining, and multi-brand pump and motor reconditioning.

Our ISO 9001:2015 certification reflects our commitment to stringent quality control, precise testing protocols, and original spare part logistics.

Peenya Works, Bangalore

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, high-performance hydraulic systems and rapid aftermarket support that minimizes equipment downtime for industrial plants across India.

Our Vision

To be recognized nationwide as the most dependable hydraulic engineering firm through technology adoption, precision craftsmanship, and service integrity.

Quality Motto

"Service ON TIME." Due to the technical complexity of hydraulic systems, machine downtime is costly. Providing rapid, competent technical support is our core commitment.

INFRASTRUCTURE

In-House Testing & Work Facilities

Our Peenya facility is equipped with specialized testing benches capable of rigorous fluid power validation.

500 LPM Test Bench

Primary hydraulic test bench rated for 500 LPM @ 300 Bar pressure driven by a 110 kW motor with Variable Frequency Drive (VFD).

200 LPM Power Unit

Dedicated testing HPU providing 200 LPM at 200 Bar pressure for valve bank and cylinder stroke testing.

700 Bar High-Pressure Rig

Ultra-high pressure test unit delivering 3 LPM @ 700 Bar for proof testing and burst pressure validation.

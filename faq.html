Frequently Asked Questions

HSM manufactures custom hydraulic power units ranging from compact 20-liter tank capacities up to large 2000-liter industrial power packs. Units are engineered with single, double, or multi-pump arrangements, custom manifold blocks, and integrated cooling systems tailored to continuous industrial duty cycles.

Our standard and custom test benches offer tank capacities from 20 to 2000 liters, motor power ratings from 0.55 to 160 kW, flow rates from 42 to 400 LPM, and testing pressure capabilities from 0.5 up to 1200 Bar. In-house rigs feature VFD motor drives and digital flow logging.

Our workshop team specializes in the reconditioning and repair of axial piston pumps, vane pumps, and orbital motors from Bosch Rexroth, Parker Hannifin (including Parker Gold Cup series), Eaton Vickers, Denison Hydraulics, Sauer Danfoss, and Yuken.

Our primary manufacturing plant and head office is located in Peenya 2nd Stage, Bangalore (No. 6 & 7, 1st Main Road, Sri Lakshmi Industrial Estate, Thigalara Palya Main Road, Bangalore – 560058). We also operate a branch presence in Pune to support regional clients.

HSM operates an ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system. All manufactured power packs, cylinders, and manifold blocks undergo 100% factory pressure and flow testing on our dedicated test benches prior to dispatch.

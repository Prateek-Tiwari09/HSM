Complete Hydraulic Field & In-House Services

From emergency troubleshooting to in-house pump remanufacturing and fluid condition monitoring, HSM Hydro Control ensures your plant operates at peak performance.

AFTERMARKET & MAINTENANCE

Our Service Ecosystem

Field engineering and workshop capabilities tailored to reduce machine downtime.

Field Service & Troubleshooting

On-site diagnostic engineers equipped with digital pressure and flow gauges to troubleshoot complex circuit faults on production lines.

In-House Pump & Motor Repair

Precision reconditioning of Rexroth, Parker Gold Cup, Eaton, Vickers, Denison, Sauer Danfoss, and Yuken axial piston & vane units.

Hydraulic Cylinder Servicing

Cylinder honing, rod re-chroming, gland bush machining, and high-pressure seal kit replacement up to 350 Bar test pressure.

Fluid Condition Monitoring

On-site particle count testing, NAS class measurement, moisture extraction, and high-efficiency offline oil filtration.

Retrofit & System Modifications

Upgrading existing power packs with energy-efficient VFD motor drives, proportional valve manifolds, and digital sensors.

Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Scheduled preventative maintenance packages, periodic oil health checks, accumulator pre-charge checks, and priority emergency response.

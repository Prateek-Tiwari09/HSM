Product Overview

Reliable hydraulic system operation depends on the quality of every auxiliary component. HSM Hydro Control supplies engineered fluid power accessories manufactured to international DIN, ISO, and SAE standards. Our line includes heavy-duty tube fittings, Viton and NBR O-rings, optical level-temperature gauges, desiccant air breathers, cast-iron bell housings, flexible spider couplings, suction strainers, high-pressure ball valves, and wire-braided hose assemblies.

Key Accessory Categories

Fittings & Flanges

DIN 2353 bite-type fittings, SAE 3000/6000 PSI split flanges, and BSP/NPT adapters.

Flexible Hose Assemblies

1SN, 2SN, 4SP, and 4SH high-pressure rubber hoses with crimped end fittings.

Tank Accessories

Fluid level-temperature indicators, filler breathers, silica gel desiccant breathers, and inspection covers.

Drive Couplings & Bell Housings

Aluminum and cast-iron bell housings with flexible jaw/spider couplings for electric motor to pump alignment.

High-Pressure Ball Valves

2-way and 3-way carbon steel ball valves rated up to 500 Bar for line isolation.

Instrumentation Sensors

Glycerin-filled pressure gauges, digital pressure transmitters, and inline temperature switches.

Technical Specifications Range

Pressure Range 0 to 630 Bar (depending on fitting/hose rating)
Temperature Range -20°C to +120°C (Standard NBR/Viton options)
Standards Compliance DIN 2353, ISO 8434, SAE J514, ISO 6162
Body Materials Carbon Steel (Zinc Plated / Phosphated), Stainless Steel SS316, Cast Iron
Media Compatibility Mineral Oils (HLP), Synthetic Fluids, Water-Glycol Fluids

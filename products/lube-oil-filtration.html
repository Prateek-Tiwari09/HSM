Product Overview

Fluid contamination is the root cause of up to 80% of hydraulic component failures. HSM Hydro Control manufactures dedicated Lube Oil and Filtration Systems designed for continuous oil purification in turbines, gearboxes, paper mills, and industrial power packs. Our systems incorporate high-efficiency micro-glass filter elements, water-removal coalescing cartridges, and online particle sensors to maintain target NAS 6 / ISO 15/13/10 cleanliness levels.

Technical Specifications

Flow Capacity 10 LPM to 300 LPM
Filtration Rating 1 Micron, 3 Micron, 6 Micron, 10 Micron, 25 Micron (Absolute Beta ≥ 200)
Target Fluid Cleanliness NAS 5 - NAS 7 / ISO 4406 Class 14/12/9
Filter Element Material Inorganic Micro-Glass Fiber with Water-Absorbing Layers
System Configuration Mobile Filtration Trolleys, Permanent Tank-Mounted Offline Loops, Lube Rigs

System Benefits

Continuous Offline Cleaning

Operates independently of the main hydraulic system without pressure spikes or flow interruption.

Water & Varnish Removal

Specialized coalescer elements remove free and emulsified water to prevent oil oxidation.

Extended Component Life

Protects expensive proportional valves, servo valves, and axial piston pumps from abrasive wear.

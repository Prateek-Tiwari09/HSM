Product Overview

HSM Hydro Control stocks and integrates a wide variety of CETOP / ISO subplate mounted hydraulic valves and screw-in cartridge valves. We supply directional control valves (NG6 / CETOP 3, NG10 / CETOP 5, NG16, NG25), pressure control valves, counterbalance valves, pilot-operated check valves, and proportional directional & flow control valves with integrated onboard electronics.

Valve Specifications & Mountings

Directional Valves CETOP 3 (D03 / NG6), CETOP 5 (D05 / NG10), CETOP 7 (D07 / NG16), CETOP 8 (NG25)
Max Operating Pressure Up to 350 Bar (5000 PSI)
Flow Rating Up to 600 LPM (depending on valve size)
Solenoid Voltages 12V DC, 24V DC, 110V AC, 220V AC with LED DIN Connectors
Proportional Controls 0-10V / 4-20mA input amplifier cards; Position transducer feedback options

Valve Categories

Directional Solenoid Valves

Spool valves in 4/2 and 4/3 spring centered or detented spool configurations.

Modular Sandwich Valves

Flow controls, check valves, and pressure reducing valves for stack mounting.

Screw-in Cartridge Valves

Compact cavity valves for integrated manifold block installation.

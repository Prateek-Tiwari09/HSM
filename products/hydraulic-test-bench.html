Product Overview

HSM Hydro Control designs and manufactures high-pressure Hydraulic Test Benches utilized by valve manufacturers, equipment rebuild workshops, and industrial quality control labs. Our test benches allow precise verification of directional control valves, relief valves, proportional valves, axial piston pumps, and heavy-duty cylinders. Equipped with digital data logging, variable frequency motor drives (VFD), and precision flow meters, our benches ensure complete compliance with international quality standards.

Technical Specifications

Tank Capacity 20 – 2000 Liters
Motor Power Rating 0.55 – 160 kW
Flow Rate Capacity 42 – 400 LPM
Testing Pressure Range 0.5 – 1200 Bar
Control Interface Manual Lever Controls / Touchscreen PLC / Digital Data Logger
Drive Control Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) for precise RPM & flow adjustment

Engineering Highlights

Extreme Pressure Capability

Ultra-high pressure intensification up to 1200 Bar for valve seat leak testing and burst pressure validation.

VFD Speed Tuning

Energy-efficient inverter drive control allows smooth motor RPM variation and precise flow measurement.

Digital Data Acquisition

Real-time pressure vs. flow graphing, electronic report generation, and automated pass/fail certificate printing.

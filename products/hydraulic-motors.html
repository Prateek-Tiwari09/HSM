Product Overview

HSM Hydro Control supplies, replaces, and repairs high-efficiency hydraulic rotary motors. Our product range includes Danfoss OMP/OMR/OMS/OMV series geroter orbital motors, Rexroth A6VM/A2FM bent-axis piston motors, and Staffa / Calzoni high-torque radial piston drives. Every motor repaired in our workshop undergoes dynamic torque and volumetric efficiency testing on our test bench before re-installation.

Technical Specifications Range

Orbital Motors (HTLS) Displacements 50 cc to 800 cc/rev; Torque up to 2200 Nm (Danfoss, Eaton Char-Lynn)
Axial Piston Motors Fixed & Variable displacement; Speed up to 6000 RPM; Pressure up to 400 Bar (Rexroth, Parker)
Radial Piston Motors Heavy-duty winch drives; Torque up to 32,000 Nm (Staffa, SAI, Calzoni)
Flange Mountings SAE A 2-Bolt, SAE B 4-Bolt, Magneto Flange, Wheel Mount Flange

Service & Supply Highlights

Direct Equivalent Replacement

Form, fit, and function replacements for hard-to-source imported motors.

Dynamic Load Testing

Full hydraulic load testing to verify starting torque and seal integrity.

Original Seals & Parts

Rebuilt strictly using OEM shaft seals, gerotor sets, and valve plates.

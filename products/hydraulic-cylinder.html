CYLINDER VISUAL PLACEHOLDER ISO Standard Heavy Duty Hydraulic Cylinder

Product Overview

HSM Hydro Control manufactures high-reliability single-acting and double-acting hydraulic cylinders for press machines, steel mills, mobile equipment, and automation lines. All cylinders are designed in compliance with ISO 6020/2 and ISO 6022 standards. In addition to standard industrial cylinders, we specialize in low-friction servo-actuators equipped with integrated LVDT position feedback transducers and low-friction PTFE sealing systems.

Technical Specifications

Bore Diameter Range 25 mm to 500 mm
Stroke Length Up to 6000 mm
Nominal Operating Pressure 160 Bar / 210 Bar / 350 Bar
Mounting Types Front Flange, Rear Clevis, Trunnion, Intermediate Trunnion, Foot Mount
Piston Rod Finish Hard Chrome Plated (Min 25-50 microns) / Nikrom / Induction Hardened
Sealing Material Polyurethane, PTFE Bronze Filled, Viton for High Temperature

Cylinder Variations

Servo Cylinders

Ultra-low friction seals and built-in magnetostrictive position sensors for closed-loop motion control.

Tie-Rod Cylinders

Compact ISO 6020/2 tie-rod cylinders ideal for machine tools and plastic injection presses.

Heavy Mill-Type Cylinders

Rugged welded steel cylinders for harsh environments in steel mills, mining, and civil engineering.

Product Overview

Custom Hydraulic Manifold Blocks consolidate multiple control valves into a single compact block, eliminating external tube connections, reducing pressure drops, and minimizing leak paths. HSM Hydro Control provides complete design and manufacturing capabilities for custom manifolds in ductile iron (SG Iron GGG40) and high-strength aluminum (Al 7075 / 6061-T6). Our 3D solid modeling software ensures interference-free cross-drilling and optimal fluid flow paths up to 420 Bar pressure.

Manufacturing Capabilities

Block Materials Ductile Iron GGG40 / SG Iron, High-Grade Aluminum 7075-T6, Carbon Steel
Working Pressure Rating Up to 350 Bar (Aluminum) / 420 Bar (Ductile Iron)
Max Block Dimensions Up to 1000 mm x 800 mm x 600 mm
Valve Cavities Sun Hydraulics, HydraForce, Rexroth, Parker, Bucher standard cavities
Surface Treatment Black Oxide Phosphating, Electroless Nickel Plating, Anodizing

Engineering Advantages

Leak-Free Design

Internal drilled galleries eliminate up to 90% of external fittings and potential leakage points.

Compact Footprint

Significantly reduces space requirements on hydraulic power packs and machine chassis.

100% Deburred & Cleaned

Thermal and mechanical deburring followed by ultra-sonic cleaning to prevent internal chip contamination.

METHODOLOGY

Our 7-Step Engineering Process

A structured, quality-controlled execution model ensuring seamless project delivery.

01

Requirement Analysis

Evaluating pressure, flow rates, duty cycles, operating environment, and plant expansion needs.

02

Circuit & Mechanical Design

Developing detailed hydraulic schematic diagrams, solid modeling, and manifold block channel routing.

03

Component Sourcing & Precision Machining

Selecting premium valves, pumps, seals, and machining manifolds using precision CNC setups.

04

Assembly & Fabrication

Structured assembly of tanks, power packs, piping, and electrical control panels in our Peenya facility.

05

High-Pressure Factory Testing

Rigorously testing flow, pressure rating, relief response, temperature stability, and seal integrity.

06

Installation & On-Site Commissioning

Erection, line flushing, pressure tuning, and handover with complete documentation.

07

Aftermarket & Lifecycle Support

Providing rapid technical field support, routine oil condition checks, and AMC coverage.
